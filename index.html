Kanimerala Village Baseline Survey & Development Intelligence

A comprehensive socio-economic, infrastructural, agricultural, and demographic baseline survey of 49 households (140 residents) in Kanimerala (Kanimerla), Mylavaram Mandal, NTR District, Andhra Pradesh, conducted under the national Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) initiative.

Mylavaram Mandal, NTR District, Andhra Pradesh (521230)
Field Survey Date: June 7, 2026
Mango Horticulture Hub & Banjara Community

Top 3 Critical Community Action Alerts

1. Acute Drainage Deficit: 67.3% of households report no underground or covered drainage, causing stagnant wastewater, severe foul odors, and mosquito infestations.
2. Clean Drinking Water Quality: While 89.8% have piped tap access, 46.9% are forced to purchase commercial 20L RO mineral water cans due to salinity and taste concerns. Repeated demand for an Overhead Water Storage Tank (OHT).
3. Public Transport & RTC Bus Isolation: Lack of direct APSRTC bus connectivity connecting Kanimerala to Mylavaram (16 km) and Nuzvid (24 km), hindering student schooling and medical emergencies.

Agrarian Challenges & Inputs

Fertilizer, land titles, and price realization
  • Chemical Dependency: Over 90% of cultivating farmers utilize Chemical Fertilizers, Pesticides, and Weedicides, escalating input costs and diminishing soil microbiome.
  • Land Title Issues ("Paatalu Problem"): Several farmers cultivate land without official Pattadar Passbooks or land deeds, barring them from institutional credit and insurance claims.
  • Borewell Depletion: Multiple residents reported drying borewells and fluctuating voltage affecting agricultural pumps.
  • Mango Post-Harvest Loss: In the absence of a cold storage or processing unit in Mylavaram, farmers are vulnerable to middleman price swings.

Infrastructure Snapshot: Energy, Cooking & Sanitation

Key achievements vs ongoing deficits in Kanimerala

100% Grid Power (0% Solar)

All 49 households have active electricity connections (averaging 24 hrs reported). However, exactly 0% have solar rooftop panels, presenting a major opportunity for PM Surya Ghar scheme adoption.

100% LPG Cooking Fuel

All surveyed households report using LPG cylinders for primary cooking, reflecting strong penetration of the Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana and Deepam schemes.

Digital Telecom Footprint

Mobile telecom access is split between Reliance Jio (75.5%) and Bharti Airtel (24.5%), providing baseline 4G internet connectivity to smartphones.

Critical Gaps & Exclusions

Where government intervention is urgently demanded

MNREGA Job Card Deficit (79.3% Excluded)

Only 29 out of 140 individuals hold active MNREGA Job Cards. 111 individuals—including agricultural laborers and coolies—are deprived of guaranteed 100 days wage employment.

Banking Exclusion (48.6% Unbanked)

68 out of 140 individuals do not have a bank account. Only 66 hold government accounts (primarily SBI & Union Bank of India). Financial literacy and PMJDY camp required.

Pension Grievances ("No Pention")

Elderly citizens reported being denied old age or widow pensions due to documentation discrepancies or portal ineligibility ("A government schemes eligible cheyyatledhu").

Healthcare Access Diagnosis

Distance to medical centers and critical needs

1. No Village Clinic / PHC: The survey records: "no government hospital facilities" in the village. Residents must travel 16 km to Mylavaram Community Health Centre (CHC) or 24 km to Nuzvid Area Hospital.

2. High Chronic NCD Burden: High rates of Hypertension (BP) and Diabetes (Sugar) without regular screening or medication supply, leading to severe outcomes like brain strokes and paralysis in elders.

3. Occupational Musculoskeletal Stress: Farmers and manual coolie laborers suffer widespread knee pain, back pain, and joint deterioration from agricultural stoop work.

4. School Nutrition Flagged: A surveyor note explicitly highlights: "food problem in schools", requiring inspection of Mid-Day Meal (Jagananna Gorumudda) quality in the local primary school.

Comprehensive Village Development Plan (VDP) (గ్రామ అభివృద్ధి ప్రణాళిక)

Actionable, phased roadmap for Gram Panchayat, UBA Institute, and NTR District Administration.

Phase 1: Immediate Interventions (0 - 6 Months)

Sanitation, Clean Water & Welfare Enrolment

Emergency Drain Desilting & Mosquito Fogging: Clean existing clogged open drains to halt wastewater stagnant pools and combat vector-borne illnesses.
Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) RO Plant Assessment: Commission community water treatment unit / overhead storage tank (OHT) so 47% of families do not need to buy private water cans.
Special Grama Ward Sachivalayam Camp: Issue MNREGA Job Cards for the 111 uncarded members and resolve rejected pension applications for elders.
Zero-Balance Bank Account Drive (PMJDY): Partner with SBI/Union Bank Mylavaram branch to open accounts for 68 unbanked members.
Phase 2: Medium-Term Upgrades (6 - 12 Months)

Infrastructure, Transport & Health Camps

APSRTC Bus Route Request: Submit petition to APSRTC NTR District Division to schedule morning and evening bus services through Kanimerala to Mylavaram and Nuzvid.
Concrete CC Road & Covered Drainage Network: Propose covered storm-water drainage and internal CC roads under MGNREGA / Panchayat Raj Engineering funds.
Monthly Mobile Medical Unit (104 Service): Regular NCD screening for Hypertension and Diabetes with free medicine dispensing in the Thanda.
Revenue Patta Passbook Adalat: Coordinate with Mylavaram Tahsildar for verification of tribal land records ("Paatalu") and housing colony allocations.
Phase 3: Long-Term Transformation (1 - 3 Years)

Horticulture Value-Addition & Solar Energy

Mango Farmer Producer Organization (FPO): Form a cooperative for Kanimerala mango growers to bypass middlemen and establish direct linkage with fruit processing units.
PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (Solar Rooftops): Transition households from 0% solar to rooftop solar grid-tie systems, generating green energy credits.
Youth Skill & Digital Centre: Leverage the 25 educated graduates in Kanimerala to run a community digital library, competitive exam coaching, and e-governance service desk.

Survey Overview

The household survey for Kanimerala village was conducted by trained student enumerator teams under the Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) participating institution on June 7, 2026. A total of 49 household questionnaires were digitally documented, capturing granular information on demographics, family composition, socio-economics, assets, housing, water, power, welfare schemes, and public grievances.
